Our Studio Story

Made by hand.
Made to be treasured.

S.bloomora was founded on a simple vision: to turn tactile, soft materials like velvet pipe cleaners into lasting pieces of heartwarming art that never wither.

Unlike fresh flowers that fade in days, our handcrafted floral pots and illuminated fairy lamps remain vibrant for years. We believe in intentional crafting, bespoke color palettes, and the enduring charm of tactile handmade gifts.
